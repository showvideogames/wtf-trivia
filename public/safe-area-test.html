Mode:

Modes (each one reloads)

1. Matched (the spec)WTF yellow page + theme-color the same yellow. Should look seamless either way. 2. StripesPage background striped pink/teal. Stripes behind the clock = the page really extends there. 3. Red theme-colorYellow page, theme-color red. A red strip = Safari paints its own area from theme-color. 4. Stripes + red theme-colorThe decisive one: stripes, red, or something else behind the clock? 5. No theme-colorWhat Safari does on its own.

How to read it

  • Stripes behind the clock (modes 2 and 4): the page background extends behind the status bar. Answer 1.
  • Flat red behind the clock (modes 3 and 4): Safari reserves a separate area and colours it from theme-color. Answer 2.
  • Flat yellow or another solid colour in mode 4: Safari reserves the area and picks the colour itself, ignoring theme-color.
  • Then scroll down: if the numbered blocks slide under the clock, page content draws behind the status bar too.
